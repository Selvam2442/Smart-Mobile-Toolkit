About Time Notes

Stopwatches are tedious. This is a rapid-entry event logger perfect for daily travel and tracking routines.

  • Quick Log: Type "Bus arrived" and hit Log to instantly save the exact time.
  • Export & Import: Easily backup time logs as JSON/CSV and restore them anytime.
