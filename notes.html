About Advanced Notes

A fast, distraction-free markdown-capable notepad and task manager. All notes are saved 100% securely offline on your device.

  • Rich Formatting: Headings, bold, italics, highlights, blockquotes, and code blocks.
  • Interactive Tasks: Insert checkboxes. Tap them right inside notes or view task completion progress on your cards!
  • Pin & Organize: Pin important notes to top, assign categories (Work, Personal, Ideas, Study), and filter with one tap.
  • Real-time Autosave: Notes save continuously in the background so you never lose your progress.
