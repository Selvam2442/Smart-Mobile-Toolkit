How to Use Exam Photo Resizer

This tool resizes photos and signatures for SSC, UPSC, IBPS, and state exam portals to exact width/height pixels and maximum KB file size limits 100% offline.

  • Choose a portal preset or enter custom dimensions in pixels.
  • Upload your photo or signature file via dropzone.
  • Click "Resize & Fit KB Limit" to auto-compress the image to meet portal requirements.
